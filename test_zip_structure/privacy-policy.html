Official Institutional Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 06, 2026

Pioneer Superior School System ("we", "our", or "us") provides a comprehensive school management application designed to facilitate academic records, attendance, fee vouchers, and institutional administration.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary for school management operations, including:

  • Student & Parent Information: Names, Father/Guardian details, student identification IDs, contact phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, and residential addresses.
  • Staff & Educator Records: Teacher names, professional designations, academic qualifications, and verified staff credentials.
  • Academic & Financial Data: Attendance logs, exam marks, report cards, fee payment status, and voucher receipts.

2. How Information Is Used

The collected information is solely used for:

  • Maintaining institutional student and teacher directories.
  • Generating fee vouchers and student identity cards.
  • Recording and tracking daily student attendance.
  • Transmitting automated academic updates and receipts via school communication channels.

3. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive school data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS in transit) and Firebase/Cloud security infrastructure. We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal information to third-party advertisers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for use by school administrators, teachers, and parents. We adhere strictly to institutional child data safety guidelines and only maintain student records authorized by the registered educational institution.
